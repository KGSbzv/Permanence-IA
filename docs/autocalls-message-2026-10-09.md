## Version anglaise à copier-coller

Hi Autocalls team, a few issues our clients hit on our white-label platform (app.permanenceia.com):

1. **Balance not refreshed after a purchase.** After buying minutes in Add credits (test user 28790, $10 = 25.64 minutes, invoice PIA-0001 paid), the "Minutes left" counter in the sidebar stays at the old value until the page is reloaded. Clients think the purchase failed. Could the balance refresh automatically after a successful payment, with a confirmation message ("25 minutes added to your balance")?
2. **No purchase confirmation.** Nothing in the app confirms the purchase (no toast, no notification, no email from the platform). Is a success notification planned, and can it be sent in the client's language?
3. **Changing the payment card is hidden.** In Add credits, the card is charged directly with no option to change it. The only way is the account menu → Billing info → Stripe portal → "Wallet" tab, which clients do not find. Could you add a "Change card" link next to the purchase button, and a "Billing & invoices" entry in the main menu?
4. **Plan and invoices not shown in "My Profile".** Clients look for their plan, trial end date, next payment and invoices in My Profile and find only name, email and timezone. Could My Profile show the current plan, status (trial / active / cancelled), trial end, next billing date, and a link to invoices and cancellation?
5. **Free accounts after signup.** A new user who skips "Choose a plan" lands on an empty dashboard with "No active plan" and 0 minutes, and never sees the 14-day free trial. Is there a setting to send new users to the Plans page right after signup while keeping "Allow free accounts" on (we sell pay-as-you-go minutes too)? We added a custom script for now.
6. **Chat credits during the trial.** Trial Plans Limits still has no "included chat credits" field: during the 14-day trial, AI written replies (WhatsApp, Messenger, website chat) stop at 0 credits. Could you add this field, or confirm the recommended way to grant credits at signup?
7. **Interface language.** We are still interested in French, Hebrew (RTL), Italian, Polish and Dutch for the client interface and system emails; please confirm whether the $1,900 per language includes the signup/login pages and the emails, and the timeline.

8. **Paused callback runs from 6 October.** About 20 test runs of our callback automations (6 October, to a blocked test number) are still paused in Automate. Could you cancel them on your side (we do not want to use "Retry")?
9. **Knowledge base 6229** has been showing "processing" for a long time. Could you check it?

Thank you!
