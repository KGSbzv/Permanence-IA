Hello Camille,

Your agent has been created, but it hasn’t received a real call yet. Three checks are usually all it takes:

  1. Give it a phone number: if you don’t have one yet, get one under “Get new phone number” (a monthly option, price shown before you buy), then in “Assistants” open the agent, “General” section, “Phone number” field.
  2. Call that number from your mobile and ask a question a customer would ask.
  3. If you’re happy with the answer, ask your phone provider to forward calls to that number, for example only when you don’t pick up. You keep your own number.

The two guides below cover each step, and the help bubble in your customer area answers your questions.
