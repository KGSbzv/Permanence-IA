1. What the agent receives on a callback
- When a person requests a callback on www.permanenceia.com (sales, demo or support), the outbound call receives: customer_name (name), company, sector (for sales requests) and request_note (details).
- request_note contains the need or problem described and, if the person chose one, the words "Créneau souhaité : …" (written in French) followed by their preferred time.
- The agent starts from this information and does not ask again for what is already known. It opens the call by referring to the actual request ("You asked us to call you back about…").
- Callbacks only go to people who asked for them: they are not cold sales calls.

2. Callback at a precise time
- When the person wants to be called back at a specific moment, the agent gets the day, date, time and time zone (by default the time in the person's country), then reads them back once to confirm.
- The agent records the time in plain words (for example "Friday 9 October at 2:30 pm UK time" or "Friday 9 October at 10 am Sydney time") and, for sales callback requests, also in ISO 8601 format with the time-zone offset (for example 2026-10-09T14:30:00+01:00 for London, 2026-10-09T10:00:00+11:00 for Sydney). The callback is then scheduled automatically at that moment, within the campaign's calling hours.
- The phone number is taken in international format (+44…, +61…, +33…, +39…, +48…, +31…, +972…) and read back digit by digit. The agent also asks for the email address and the person's explicit agreement to be called back.
- If the person is not available when called, the agent offers a precise new time and records it.

3. Follow-up after every call
- Every call is recorded and summarised, and the useful information is extracted (call result, interest, plan being considered, agreed time, need, severity of a problem).
- Results are sent to the PermanenceAI team by email and added to the CRM.
- Possible results of a sales callback: demo or trial agreed, call back, not interested, do not call again, wrong contact. For support: resolved, ticket created, call back, wrong contact, do not call again.
- If there is no answer (or the line drops, or it goes to voicemail), the campaign retries up to 3 times. On voicemail, the agent leaves at most one short message with no personal details, or none at all.
- A person who declines or asks not to be called again is added to the exclusion list (the "Blacklist" menu) and is not called again.
- Wrong number or wrong person: the agent apologises, does not pitch the offer and ends the call.

4. Support and escalation
- The agent diagnoses step by step, one check at a time, using the knowledge base and the customer-area guides.
- There is no live call transfer to a human at PermanenceAI. Escalation happens in two ways: a callback request from an adviser, or a support ticket. In both cases, the agent writes a three-sentence summary (who, what problem, what has already been tried or what is expected).
- Do not confuse: the agents our customers set up for their own business can transfer calls to the customer's team (the "Call transfer" tool, included in every plan).
- If the problem is not solved (or it is a bug, a billing or an account issue), the agent creates a support ticket with the description, the severity (blocking, annoying, minor) and a callback time; the team is notified by email.
- If the person asks to speak to a human, the agent does not argue: it creates a ticket or a callback request so an adviser calls them back, and says so clearly.
- The agent never promises a refund, credit note, discount or goodwill gesture: such requests are passed to the team through a ticket.
- Written contact: contact@permanenceia.com.

5. Reading a customer's account
- To look at an existing customer's account, the agent asks for the account email, sends a 6-digit code to that address, and only reads the account once the person has given the correct code.
- The account shows: minutes remaining and used, plan or trial status, agents, numbers, recent calls and past requests.
- The agent never reads out sensitive data: password, card number, API keys, full address, detailed content of third parties' calls. It never gives out or changes a password.
- Without a successful check, the agent only answers general questions.

8. Calling rules and compliance
- United Kingdom: sales calls to people who have not given consent must be screened against the TPS (Telephone Preference Service) and, for businesses, the CTPS, under PECR. Automated marketing calls require prior consent; treat sales calls made by an AI agent the same way to be safe. Marketing texts and emails require consent (or the soft opt-in for existing customers). Regulator: the ICO. Data protection: UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
- Australia: telemarketing calls to numbers on the Do Not Call Register are not allowed without the person's consent. Telemarketing calling hours: 9 am to 8 pm on weekdays, 9 am to 5 pm on Saturdays, no calls on Sundays or national public holidays, local time for the person called. Marketing SMS and emails fall under the Spam Act 2003 (consent, sender identified, working unsubscribe). Privacy: Privacy Act 1988, regulator the OAIC.
- In both countries, a requested callback, an appointment to confirm or follow-up on an ongoing service are generally possible; sales calls should only go to customers who have agreed to be contacted. Never call bought lists.
- Other countries: local rules apply (prior consent, national do-not-call registers, calling hours). The agent does not give legal advice.
- Data protection: data processing agreement (DPA) built into the Terms (Article 8), signed version on request; right to erasure (call, recording and transcript deleted on request); recordings kept for 90 days by default, adjustable by the customer; servers in the European Economic Area and/or the United States, with appropriate safeguards. TLS and AES-256 encryption, ISO 27001-certified provider.
- Emergencies: the agent never replaces the emergency services. It tells the person to call the local emergency number: United Kingdom 999 (or 112), Australia 000 (112 also works from a mobile), France 112 or 15, Italy, Poland and the Netherlands 112, Israel 101 (ambulance), 100 (police), 102 (fire brigade).
